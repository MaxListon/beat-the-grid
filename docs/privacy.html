Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Beat the Grid is a personal data-analysis project by Maxwell Liston. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you visit.

The short version

  • This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • If you send a message through the contact form, I use it only to reply to you.
  • I never sell or share your information.

Information stored in your browser

Two small preferences are saved in your browser's local storage, never sent anywhere, and never used to identify you: your light/dark theme choice, and whether you've dismissed the privacy notice. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages, and may keep them in server logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Code libraries and map data

The charts and map load open-source files from cdnjs (run by Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. These services receive your IP address and browser details to deliver the files, as any website you visit does. They don't receive anything else from this site.

Visitor statistics

If visitor statistics are switched on, they use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused service that sets no cookies and stores no personal data. It counts page views, referring sites, browser type, screen size and approximate country. Your IP address is used only to estimate the country and is not stored.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email, optional company and message you enter are delivered to me by Formspree, which forwards them to my email. I use them only to reply to you and delete them on request. A hidden field and a timing check filter out automated spam. Neither collects any information about you.

Your choices

You can ask me to delete any message you've sent, or ask what I hold about you, by writing through the contact form or on LinkedIn. This site isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. The full history is public in the project repository.
